Privacy Policy for Air Dice This policy applies to the Air Dice mobile application (“App”). Air Dice is a local airplane battle board game for one to four players on the same device. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used. 1. Information We Collect Air Dice does not ask you to create an account and does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, location data, or gameplay analytics. Names or labels entered for local players, game state, dice rolls, and match results are used only to run the game on your device. The App includes game sound effects. Audio is played locally; the App does not record or access your microphone. 2. How Information Is Used Information entered or generated during a match is used to display and operate that match. The App does not use it for advertising, profiling, or analytics. 3. Third-Party Services and Sharing The current version of Air Dice does not include analytics, advertising, account, or cloud-service SDKs. We do not sell or share your personal information. The App does not send gameplay information to us or to third parties. 4. Data Retention and Deletion Game information is handled locally by the App and is not retained on our servers. Information present only during a match is cleared when the App or match is reset or closed. You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling it, subject to your device’s operating-system behavior. 5. Data Security Because the App does not transmit personal information to us, we do not maintain a server-side collection of your game data. Keep your device secured using the protections provided by its operating system. 6. Children’s Privacy Air Dice does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. If you believe information has been sent to us in connection with the App, please contact us through the project page below. 7. Your Choices and Rights You can stop using the App or remove its local data through your device settings. Since we do not collect personal information on our servers, we do not have user accounts or server-side personal data to provide, correct, or delete. You may contact us with privacy questions or requests. 8. Changes to This Policy We may update this policy if the App’s features or data practices change.
